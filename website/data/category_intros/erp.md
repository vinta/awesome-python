Odoo's CRM, e-commerce, inventory, and point-of-sale apps work as one Python ERP on PostgreSQL. Install the apps you need, and write a module for the rest.

Pick Odoo when you want your company's business apps in one system that your developers can change: [its logic tier is all Python](https://www.odoo.com/documentation/latest/developer/tutorials/server_framework_101/01_architecture.html), and every feature ships as a module. It comes in two editions: Community is open source, and Enterprise is licensed, adding [extra modules that run on the Community server](https://www.odoo.com/documentation/latest/administration/on_premise/source.html) plus services like support and upgrades.

Run Odoo [from source](https://www.odoo.com/documentation/latest/developer/tutorials/setup_guide.html), as its docs recommend for developers. Then build your first module in the [Server framework 101](https://www.odoo.com/documentation/latest/developer/tutorials/server_framework_101.html) tutorial: a directory with at least a `__manifest__.py` and an `__init__.py`.

Before you write a module, [check whether Odoo already covers the business case](https://www.odoo.com/documentation/latest/developer/tutorials/server_framework_101/02_newapp.html). When an app comes close, build on it from your own module. With `_inherit` and no new `_name`, your module [extends a model](https://www.odoo.com/documentation/latest/developer/reference/backend/orm.html#extension) that another module defines, adding fields and methods. [View inheritance](https://www.odoo.com/documentation/latest/developer/tutorials/server_framework_101/12_inheritance.html#view-inheritance) layers your changes on top of the app's views instead of overwriting them.

In production, [serve Odoo over HTTPS](https://www.odoo.com/documentation/latest/administration/on_premise/deploy.html#https) through a reverse proxy, since it sends login data in cleartext. Keep your custom modules lean: a database [can't move to a new major version](https://www.odoo.com/documentation/latest/administration/upgrade.html) until they're ported. Rehearse each upgrade on a test copy before production.
